◆ The story behind the brand

Built by a student.
Worn by anyone who moves.

JADEITE was born out of a frustration Jad Rbii knew all too well — spending hours training, but never finding gymwear that felt both premium and purposeful. Every option was either a fast-fashion knockoff or a legacy brand charging luxury prices for mediocre quality.

At 17, based in Dubai, Jad decided to stop waiting for someone else to solve it. He spent months researching the global activewear market, interviewing peers across the UAE, and studying the gap between what Gen Z athletes needed and what the industry was offering.

The result is JADEITE — a brand rooted in craft, compression, and clarity. Designed for people who take their training seriously, but also want to look good outside the gym.

01

Identified the gap

No brand was designing premium gymwear for young, style-conscious athletes at an accessible price point — especially in the GCC.

02

Researched the market

Surveyed 200+ peers. Analysed 12 competitor brands. Studied supply chains, fabric technology, and consumer psychology across 6 months.

03

Built the brand

Designed the product range, created the visual identity, launched the website, and prepared JADEITE for its first public drop — all before graduating.

◆ Research & Insights

The data behind the drop.

Before designing a single piece, Jad spent six months researching the activewear landscape — market size, consumer behaviour, competitor weaknesses, and the specific demands of the Gen Z athlete.

Global activewear market
$600B+
Projected market size by 2030, growing at 6.7% CAGR — one of the fastest-growing segments in fashion.
Gen Z dissatisfaction
73%
Of surveyed 16–22 year olds said existing gymwear brands don't reflect their identity or values.
Price sensitivity
$55–$85
The sweet spot — what young athletes are willing to spend for quality activewear, per Jad's 200-person survey.
MENA opportunity
$8B
Estimated UAE + GCC premium sportswear market — massively underpenetrated by emerging independent brands.
◆ The problem vs. the solution

What the market offers

  • Fast-fashion gymwear that loses shape after 10 washes
  • Legacy brands (Nike, Lululemon) at prices teens can't afford
  • Generic colourways — black, grey, navy — nothing distinctive
  • No brand rooted in the GCC or designed for that climate
  • Poorly constructed seams that chafe during training
  • Marketing that ignores young, male gymwear consumers
VS

What JADEITE delivers

  • Premium compression fabrics engineered to hold shape long-term
  • Mid-premium price point: quality without the legacy markup
  • Signature jade-inspired palette — distinctive and wearable
  • Designed with the Dubai athlete and UAE market in mind
  • Bonded seams and four-way stretch for unrestricted movement
  • Inclusive range — women's and men's from day one
◆ Key insights from Jad's research
Consumer behaviour
Style drives purchase more than performance specs
68% of surveyed young athletes said they choose gymwear primarily on how it looks, not on technical specs. Function matters — but aesthetics close the sale.
Brand loyalty
Gen Z switches brands 3× faster than Millennials
Without strong visual identity and community, activewear brands lose Gen Z customers in under two years. JADEITE is built around a distinct identity from day one.
Market gap
No premium indie brand owns the GCC gymwear space
The UAE has a booming fitness culture — 400+ gyms in Dubai alone — but zero homegrown activewear brands at a premium tier. JADEITE targets that gap directly.
Sustainability
72% of young consumers will pay more for sustainable products
Jad's research found strong willingness to pay a premium for sustainable fabrics and ethical sourcing — especially among female athletes aged 16–24.
◆ Research timeline
1
Month 1–2

Market sizing & competitor analysis

Mapped the global activewear market. Analysed 12 brands in depth — pricing, positioning, fabric quality, target demographic, and geographic reach.

2
Month 2–3

Consumer survey — 200+ respondents

Surveyed peers across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and online communities. Identified the price sweet spot, key pain points, and the aesthetic preferences of young athletes.

3
Month 3–4

Fabric & manufacturing research

Studied compression fabric technology, sustainable material alternatives, and global manufacturing options to understand what's possible at the target price point.

4
Month 5–6

Brand development & product design

Translated all research into JADEITE — the brand identity, colour palette, product range, and go-to-market strategy. Built the website and prepared for launch.
